# Playing Muve2000

## Play an instrument or kit

1. Load **Muve2000** in a Schwung sound-generator slot and open its editor. Turn **Jog** to browse named sounds in the current bank. Play the pads or a MIDI keyboard as you browse.
2. Press **Click** and choose **Instruments**, **Drums** or **SFX Kits**. Choose **Bank Variations** to hear the available alternatives for the current program. Names and available variations come from your own ROMs.
3. Choose **Settings** to select the MIDI part, bank MSB, bank LSB or octave. Octave transposes incoming notes by up to four octaves in either direction. The file player has its own engine and plays the notes written in the file.

The eight encoders control volume, pan, cutoff, resonance, attack, release, reverb send and chorus send. Touch a knob to hear its value. Turn it to edit the selected part. The two sends feed the effects selected in the current XG setup.

## Play a MIDI arrangement

Open **Muve2000 MIDI Player** under **Tools**. Click **Open MIDI File**, turn Jog to a folder or file, and click to open it. The browser accepts MID, MIDI and KAR. On the transport screen, **Click** or **Menu** opens the player menu.

- **Play** starts or pauses playback. **Shift + Play** returns to the beginning. **Loop** toggles repeat.
- On the transport screen, turn **Jog** to seek five seconds per step. Hold **Shift** for 30 seconds. Seeking restores earlier MIDI setup. A note already held at the destination may need its next note-on to sound.
- **Shift + Click** on the transport screen announces position, render load, voices, audio gaps, lost MIDI and the current Live edit delay when applicable.
- **Back** hides the player and allows playback to continue. **Shift + Back** exits it and frees its memory.

## Choose a playback mode

**Render First** is the initial mode. Press Play to prepare a file under ten minutes in memory, then playback starts. The render follows the file's tempo and can take longer than the song. Shift + Click reads progress. Play cancels preparation. Use this mode for listening, seeking or looping a finished mix.

Choose **Live** under **Playback Mode** to mix parts or play extra notes during the song. The player collects about one second of audio before starting and can build up to six seconds of reserve. This kept the dense passage in F-Cool playing cleanly on Move. Part edits and incoming notes are heard after audio already in the queue. **Master Volume** and Stop act on the current output. For playing a keyboard by itself, use the generator for its shorter response.

Both modes use the file's tempo, including tempo changes. The player remembers the mode you choose. If Live overloads on a file under ten minutes, the player offers a one-off Render First retry.

## Mix the four ports

Open **Parts** and use **Shift + Jog** to choose port A, B, C or D. The lower 16 pads mute parts 1–16 on that port. Hold **Shift** and press a pad to solo a part. The eight knobs trim parts 1–8, or 9–16 with Shift. Touch a knob to hear its level. Trims work alongside the volume changes in the MIDI file. Use Live to hear mixer changes during playback.
